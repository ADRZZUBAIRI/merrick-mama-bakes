Meet Nicole • Merrick Mama Bakes

Made with love,
Baked for Health

Hi, I'm Nicole! What began in my home kitchen as a quest to bake wholesome, gentle-on-the-stomach treats for my family quickly turned into Merrick Mama Bakes.

We love all the nostalgic comfort treats we grew up on: pop-tarts, warm cinnamon rolls, chewy cookies, and crusty artisanal boules. But supermarket versions are packed with bleached flours, preservatives, and seed oils.

Every bake in our micro-bakery uses long wild sourdough fermentation to break down gluten and phytic acid, making each bite deeply digestible, naturally flavorful, and good for the soul.

How Nicole Bakes

The 36-hour slow craft

No commercial shortcuts, no instant baker's yeast, no dough conditioners. Pure time, wild cultures, and deliberate care.

1

Feed & Autolyse

We feed our energetic starter with organic stoneground flours and hydrate the grains gently to awaken gluten naturally.

2

Gentle Stretch & Fold

Every 30 minutes, dough is lovingly lifted and folded to build airy pocket structures without machine violence.

3

24h Cold Retardation

Bannetons rest overnight in the chill. Lactobacilli break down complex starches, imparting signature tang and easy digestion.

4

Cast Iron Blistered Bake

Scored with artistic razor cuts and steamed at 475°F to form shatteringly crisp blistered crusts and tender open crumbs.
